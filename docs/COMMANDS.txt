GENERAL NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Currency is shown as "₹ UN". Internally money is stored in integer sub-units
  where 100 units = ₹1, so all amounts are whole numbers. Example: /pay @user 500
  means 500 units = ₹5.00.
- Every user is auto-created on first interaction. New users receive a
  welcome grant of ₹500 (50,000 units) in their wallet (admin-configurable).
- Most commands work in private chats, groups AND supergroups. Channels and
  bots are ignored. /start and /help are private-chat only.
- Target resolution (for any @user / user argument, everywhere in the bot):
      reply-to message  >  numeric Telegram user id  >  UNOITACHI UID
      (UID-xxxxxx)  >  @username.
  Unknown users are auto-registered when targeted — a user does NOT need to
  have started the bot first.
- All money movements produce an audit transaction with a unique #txid.
  /transactions only shows your last 10 transfer (PAY) logs; other types
  are still recorded in the database.
- Every /pay and /rob target is protected by a centralized guard: you can
  never send money to yourself, to the bot itself, or to any Telegram bot
  account.

PERMISSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two ranks control the bot:
  1. OWNER      — the numeric OWNER_ID set in .env. Full access to everything,
                  and bypasses per-group "admin commands off" restrictions.
  2. SUDO ADMIN — users added by the owner with /addsudo. Access to all admin
                  commands except the owner-only ones below.

Permission split on admin commands:
  SUDO + OWNER : everything marked (owner + sudo) and every plain admin
                 command in this reference.
  OWNER ONLY   : /addsudo /rsudo /clear /restore /recover /restorecase
                 /securityset.

Permissions are ALWAYS resolved from numeric Telegram user ids, never
usernames, so a username change cannot grant or revoke access.

Group admins on Telegram (group admin rights) have NO special bot powers.

DAILY INCOME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/interestbank    Claim daily bank income (extra, on top of the automatic
                 bank interest). Income builds up every 24h on your current
                 bank balance.
/interestasset   Claim daily asset income — every 24h on your current asset
                 portfolio value.
/stockinterest   Claim daily stock income — every 24h on your current stock
                 portfolio value.

    Income is never lost: if you do not claim, it keeps stacking day after
    day. Claiming pays out EVERY unclaimed day at once and then starts a
    fresh 24h window. Each source has its own clock, so you can claim all
    three independently. Rates are configured by admins via /setincome.
    Examples:
        /interestbank   -> claims N days of bank income in one go
        /interestasset  -> claims N days of asset income in one go
        /stockinterest  -> claims N days of stock income in one go

RESALE MARKET — your own listings
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can SELL assets you own to other users at any price you choose. Each
listing gets its own unique LISTING ID (e.g. LST-1A2B3C4D) — it is never the
asset's ID. Buyers buy the WHOLE listing (all of its quantity).

/listasset SYMBOL quantity price
    Create a listing offering quantity of SYMBOL at "price" per unit.
    Your quantity is reserved in the listing (it stays in your holdings but
    cannot be sold elsewhere until the listing closes). A configurable
    listing fee (default 0) is charged. Logs the fee (if any).
    Example: /listasset GOLD 0.5 82000

/listings [SYMBOL] [page]
    Browse the active resale market. Optionally filter by SYMBOL and paginate
    with ◀ ▶ buttons. Example: /listings GOLD

/buylisting LISTING_ID
    Buy an entire listing. The money moves from your wallet to the seller's
    wallet and the asset moves to your holdings atomically. Logs
    ASSET_LISTING_BUY (buyer) and ASSET_LISTING_SALE (seller).
    You cannot buy your own listing. Example: /buylisting LST-1A2B3C4D

/mylistings
    All your listings with their current status (active/pending/sold/cancelled).

/cancellisting LISTING_ID
    Cancel one of your ACTIVE listings; the quantity is released back to you.
    Example: /cancellisting LST-1A2B3C4D

/rmlisting LISTING_ID
    Same as /cancellisting — remove one of YOUR OWN listings. You can only
    remove listings you created; other users' listings are never touched.
    Example: /rmlisting LST-1A2B3C4D

GAMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All games require money in your WALLET (bet is locked atomically), and every
game has a cooldown (default 60s). You can only have ONE active session per
game. A failed bet only loses the bet; no money is ever duplicated.

/fly low|medium|high amount
    Plane game. Choose difficulty, bet an amount. Win probability and
    multiplier depend on the difficulty (see /flyset table below).
    Example: /fly high 1000

/mines amount
    Minesweeper-style 6x6 board with a configurable number of bombs (default
    5). A bet is locked; reveal safe tiles to increase the payout multiplier,
    or "cash out" early. Hit a bomb and you lose the bet. Sessions expire
    after 5 minutes (default) and are settled as a loss. Played via an
    inline keyboard (buttons). Example: /mines 2000

/bet amount
    Instant coin-flip bet with a fixed win probability and multiplier
    (default 50% / 2.0x). Example: /bet 1000

/colour amount
    Prediction game. Pick ONE size (BIG/MEDIUM/SMALL), ONE colour (GREEN/RED/
    VIOLET/BLUE) and ONE number (0-9) on the inline keyboard, then press
    🎯 PLAY. The result number is rolled AFTER you press PLAY (independent of
    your picks). The result's colour and size are derived from the number:
        colour:  0=VIOLET, 1/3/5/7=RED, 2/4/6/8=GREEN, 9=BLUE
        size:    0-3=SMALL, 4-6=MEDIUM, 7-9=BIG
    A category matches when your pick equals the result's value. Payout scales
    with the number of matched categories (multipliers configurable via
    /colourset; defaults 0 / 1.5x / 4x / 8x) and is capped by max_multiplier
    and max_payout, so wins stay bounded. 0 matches = loss of the bet.
    Sessions expire after 15 seconds (default) and are settled as a loss.
    Only the player who started the game can use its buttons.
    Example: /colour 1000

/aviator amount
    Aviator crash game. Your bet is locked and a single game message with a
    💰 CASH OUT button is sent. The multiplier climbs a slow curve (~2s
    between updates, ~60s max round); press CASH OUT before the plane crashes
    to win bet x multiplier (profit = payout - bet). If the plane crashes
    first the bet is lost. The crash moment is random and independent of the
    player. Sessions that are never resolved expire after 60s (default) and
    are settled as a loss. The game message is edited in place (never spammed)
    and edits are UI-only — a failed edit or FloodWait never changes the
    settlement. Amounts use the global parser (1000 / 10k / 1m / 1 lakh / 1
    crore). By default max_bet and max_payout are unlimited (you may bet your
    whole wallet and every generated payout is paid out); both are adjustable
    via /aviatorset. Only the player who started the game can use the button.
    Example: /aviator 1000

EMOJI GAMES (Telegram animated dice)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Solo rounds use Telegram's real animated dice — the bot sends the emoji, waits
~1 second, then sends a NEW HTML message with the result (the dice message is
never edited). Win rules and multipliers are admin-configurable per game
(defaults below).

Games / defaults:
    🎳 ball      1-6, WIN when roll >= 5 (gte), multiplier 1.0x
    🎯 arrow     1-6, WIN when roll == 6 (eq),  multiplier 1.5x
    🏀 basketball 1-5, WIN when roll >= 4 (gte), multiplier 1.5x
    ⚽ football   1-5, WIN when roll == 5 (eq),  multiplier 1.5x
    🎰 slot      1-64, pays on matching reels (see /emojigameinfo slot)
    🎲 dice      1-6, WIN when roll == 6 (eq),  multiplier 2.0x

Single-player (bet locked at start, WIN = bet + bet*multiplier, LOSS = 0):
/sball amount
/sarrow amount
/sbasketball amount
/sfootball amount
/sslot amount
/sdice amount
    Example: /sball 500

Duels (1v1, 4-digit Game ID lobby):
/ball amount
/arrow amount
/basketball amount
/football amount
/slot amount
/dice amount
/join CODE
    /ball amount      -> creates a lobby with a 4-digit code (same for all duels)
    /join CODE        -> join the lobby; both players' bets are locked
    A SINGLE emoji is rolled. The game's value range is split: upper half wins
    for the creator, lower half for the joiner; the exact middle value draws
    (only on odd-sized ranges like 1-5). Winner takes the pot (2x the bet);
    draw refunds both bets. Lobbies expire after 5 minutes (default) and the
    creator's bet is auto-refunded.
    Example:
        Player A: /ball 1000   -> Game ID: 4821
        Player B: /join 4821   -> one roll; winner takes ₹2,000.

Cooldowns are per game and per user; for a duel BOTH players must be off
cooldown. You can only have ONE active emoji game at a time.

BLACKJACK (USER VS BOT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/blackjack amount
    Simple 2-card showdown vs the bot. No hit/stand/split/double/insurance.
        Cards:  2-10 = face value, J/Q/K = 10, A = 11 (or 1 if it would bust).
        Outcome: higher total wins; equal totals = DRAW and the bet returns.
    WIN payout = bet + bet * multiplier (default 1.0x -> 2x). Example:
    /blackjack 1000

CRIME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/rob @user | /rob user_id | /rob UID-xxxxxx | reply to a user + /rob
    Try to rob another user's BANK (not wallet). Uses the reply-based target
    resolution. Outcome is random:
        - success: you steal a percentage of the victim's bank, clamped to a
          minimum/maximum (default 10%, min ₹1, max ₹5,000);
        - fail:    the police catch you and you get nothing.
    Either way the 60s cooldown applies. The victim is sent a private
    notification. Robber and victim both get audit transactions (ROB/ROBBED).
    Example:  /rob @sangam
              (reply to a message) /rob
    You cannot rob yourself, the bot, or another bot account, and the victim
    must have money in their bank.

PROMO CODES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Promo codes let admins give away currency, stocks and assets. Users redeem a
promo by simply TYPING the code as a normal text message in DM, groups or
supergroups. Detection runs automatically; the code must appear as a 3-20
letter/number token in the message. Only valid, active codes are processed;
ordinary messages and unknown/inactive codes do nothing. The explicit
<code>/redeem</code> command is also supported and uses the same engine.

/redeem CODE
    Explicitly redeem a promo code (same engine as auto-detection). Any user.
    Only valid, active codes are redeemed; unknown or inactive codes report an
    error here (and are silently ignored by auto-detection).

BANK & TAX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/setinterest rate
    Bank interest percentage paid per 24 hours. Valid 0-100. Default 2%.
    Example: /setinterest 3

/settax rate
    Withdrawal tax percentage. Valid 0-100. Default 5%. Tax is collected into
    the pool, not destroyed, and redistributed monthly. Example: /settax 4

/setincome bank|asset|stock rate
    Daily income percentage per 24 hours for a claim source. Valid 0-100.
    Defaults: bank 2%, asset 1%, stock 1%. The income is claimed by users
    via /interestbank, /interestasset and /stockinterest.
    Example: /setincome asset 1.5

/addtax system rate
    Set a transaction tax (percent) for one system. The tax is automatically
    collected into the tax pool when that system's transactions happen.
    Systems:
        bank     - withdrawal tax (same as /settax)
        assets   - asset buys and sells
        stocks   - stock buys and sells
        payments - /pay transfers (charged to the sender, on top of amount)
        mines    - mines payouts
        fly      - fly payouts
        bet      - bet payouts
    Rates are percent per transaction, valid 0-100. Default 0% (except bank
    which defaults to 5%). Use /taxinfo to view all rates.
    Examples:
        /addtax assets 2        (2% on every asset buy/sell)
        /addtax payments 1      (1% on every /pay, sender pays amount + 1%)
        /addtax mines 5         (5% withheld from mines winnings)

/dtax
    Manually distribute the CURRENT tax pool to the monthly Top-10 earners
    using the configured rank percentages. Unlike the automatic month-end
    job, a manual run does not mark the month as distributed, so the monthly
    auto-distribution still runs. Every manual run is audited in the
    tax_distributions collection under its own key.
    Example: /dtax

/taxinfo
    Show the tax pool size and every per-system transaction tax rate.

/banksettings
    Show current interest rate, withdrawal tax and tax pool size.

/track TRANSACTION_ID
    Look up one transaction by its id and show the full audit record: id,
    user, type, amount, balance before/after, metadata and timestamp.
    Example: /track 3f2a1b4c9d8e7f6a

GAMES — FLY (admin)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/flyset low|medium|high field value
    Change ONE fly difficulty setting.
    Fields:  min_mult | max_mult | risk | win_prob | cooldown | min_bet | max_bet
    Validation:
        min_mult/max_mult : 0 to 1000, and min_mult <= max_mult
        risk              : percent, 0 to 100

        min_bet/max_bet   : >= 0, and min_bet <= max_bet

    Example: /flyset high win_prob 0.40

    Current defaults per difficulty (cooldown 60s everywhere):

PROMO CODES (admin)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Promo codes let admins give away currency, stocks and assets. Users redeem a
promo by simply TYPING the code as a normal text message in DM, groups or
supergroups. Detection runs automatically; the code must appear as a 3-20
letter/number token in the message. The explicit /redeem CODE command is also
supported and uses the same redemption engine.

/addpromo CODE EXPIRY LIMIT REWARD [REWARD...]
    Create a promo code. Owner/sudo only.
    CODE:    3-20 letters/numbers (case-insensitive, non-alphanumerics ignored).
    EXPIRY:  lifetime/unlimited/forever, or a number + min/hr/day/week/month/
             year, e.g. 10min, 2hr, 7days, 30days, 1week, 2months.
    LIMIT:   total redemption limit, or 'unlimited'.
    REWARD:  one or more tokens, one per reward:
                 rs:AMOUNT            currency, e.g. rs:500 = ₹500
                 stock:SYMBOL:QTY     stock holdings, e.g. stock:BTC:0.01
                 asset:ASSET_ID:QTY   asset holdings, e.g. asset:AST-00021:1
    Every user can redeem a promo at most once (per-user limit is always 1).
    Examples:
        /addpromo ITACHI500 7days 100 rs:500
        /addpromo MEGA 30days 500 rs:100 stock:BTC:0.01 asset:AST-00021:1
        /addpromo VIP lifetime unlimited rs:500 stock:BTC:0.1 asset:AST-00021:2

/rmpromo CODE
    Disable a promo. Redemption history is kept; the code stops working.

/editpromo CODE FIELD VALUE [VALUE...]
    Edit an existing promo without touching redemption history.
    Fields:
        expiry VALUE     new expiry, e.g. 7days or lifetime
        limit VALUE      new total limit, e.g. 50 or unlimited
        active on|off    enable/disable the promo
        reward TOKENS    replace the whole reward bundle
    Example: /editpromo ITACHI500 limit 500
             /editpromo ITACHI500 reward rs:1000 stock:BTC:0.02

/promoinfo CODE
    Promo details: status, expiry (with time left), uses, per-user limit and
    the reward bundle.

/promolist [status] [page]
    List promos newest first. status: active (default view filters with 'all'
    accepted too), expired, inactive, or all. 10 per page.

/promostats CODE
    Redemption statistics: total redemptions, unique users, remaining uses,
    totals given per reward type and the last redemption time.

    Expired promos are deactivated automatically every minute.

USERS (admin)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/freeze @user
    Freeze a user. They keep their money but cannot spend it (games, pay,
    rob, withdraw are blocked). Their balance stays intact.

/unfreeze @user
    Remove a freeze.

/ban @user
    Full economy ban: the user cannot use any economy command at all.
    You cannot ban yourself or the owner.

/unban @user
    Remove a ban.

/leaderban @user
    Hide a user from EVERY leaderboard (/leader, /topbank and the monthly
    tax distribution). This is a VISIBILITY toggle only — it does not ban,
    freeze or otherwise affect their economy. The owner can never be hidden.
    Example: /leaderban @sangam

/leaderunban @user
    Restore a user's visibility on every leaderboard.
    Example: /leaderunban @sangam

/clearlb AMOUNT USER_COUNT
    Set the wallet of each of the top USER_COUNT net-worth users (fresh read,
    exclusions honored) to EXACTLY AMOUNT — it is the FINAL balance, not a
    subtraction. Example: /clearlb 100 4 sets the wallet of the Top 4 users to
    exactly ₹100 each (regardless of how much they had before).
    Every reset runs through the atomic economy engine (bank, stocks and
    assets are untouched) and writes an audit transaction with
    balance_before and balance_after (ADMIN_REMOVE when reduced,
    ADMIN_GIVE when raised). Users already at AMOUNT are skipped; the owner is
    always skipped. USER_COUNT must be 1-20. AMOUNT 0 is allowed (the engine
    supports a zero-balance reset).
    Example: /clearlb 100 10

/userinfo @user
    Full account report: wallet, bank, net worth, total earned/spent,
    monthly rank, transaction count, and BANNED/FROZEN badges.

SECURITY (admin)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/gban @user | /gban user_id | reply to a user + /gban
    Globally ban a user. They cannot use the bot in any chat.
    Sudo + owner only. Also sets the local economy ban automatically.
    Optionally add a reason as the 3rd argument; a reason containing
    "exploit" or "critical" also creates a high-severity security case.
    Example: /gban @sangam 0day vulnerability

/ungban @user | /ungban user_id | reply to a user + /ungban
    Remove a global ban. Sudo + owner only.
    Example: /ungban @sangam

/clear USER | /clear user_id | reply to a user + /clear
    Owner only. Backup + reset a user's economy:
        1. snapshot the user's economy,
        2. create a recovery dump and return a RECOVERY ID (dump id),
        3. reset their wallet to the fresh-account starting balance.
    The returned recovery ID is used with /restore or /recover to restore
    the user's economy. This does NOT ban or freeze the user.
    Example: /clear @sangam

/restore DUMP-ID
    Owner only. Restore a user's economy from a security dump. Each dump can
    only be restored once.
    Example: /restore DMP-1A2B3C4D

/recover DUMP-ID
    Owner only. Same engine as /restore — recover a user from a security dump.
    Example: /recover DMP-1A2B3C4D

/restorecase CASE-ID
    Owner only. Restore a user from a security case. Only works for cases
    that carry a recoverable (recovery/restore) action.
    Example: /restorecase CASE-XXXXXXXX

/dumpinfo
    Sudo + owner only. List the most recent security dumps (up to 10) with
    dump id, user count, type, creation time and status.

/dumps
    Sudo + owner only. List ALL security dumps with basic info: dump id, type,
    user count, status, creation time.

/securityset
    Owner only. View the current security configuration:
        • Secret detection: enabled/disabled
        • Global ban on exploit: enabled/disabled
        • Clear recovery balance: [number]

BROADCAST (admin)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/bgc
    Owner/sudo only. Reply to a message (text, photo, video, GIF, document or
    audio, formatting preserved) and send it to every registered group.
    The replied message is serialized by the central broadcast extractor:
    native Telegram entities (bold/italic/underline/strikethrough/spoiler/
    code/pre/links/mentions/custom emoji/blockquote) are preserved exactly,
    literal HTML tags (like <b>Bold</b> or <tg-spoiler>…) are rendered via
    HTML parse mode, media spoilers (has_spoiler) are kept for photo/video/GIF,
    and only URL inline buttons from the source message are carried over
    (callback buttons are never replayed). Because the message is serialized
    up front, the broadcast works even if the source message is deleted later.
    Groups are registered AUTOMATICALLY when the bot joins them or receives a
    message in them — no manual /setchat needed. Shows a confirmation with
    Confirm/Cancel buttons before sending; a large broadcast waits for inline
    confirmation so nothing is sent twice.
    The requester is excluded from the target list.
    Example: (reply to a message) /bgc

/bdm
    Owner/sudo only. Same as /bgc (same serialized formatting, media, spoiler
    and URL buttons) but sends to every user who has STARTED the bot (sent
    /start in a private chat) via DM. Bot accounts, banned and frozen users
    are excluded, and the requester is skipped. Sends to a user who blocked
    the bot are counted as "blocked", not failures.
    Example: (reply to a message) /bdm

ADMIN PANEL (admin)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/adminpanel
    Owner/sudo only. Opens an interactive inline-button menu that groups every
    admin command by category (economy, bank & taxes, loans, markets, promos,
    games, users, broadcast, system & recovery). Every button re-verifies the
    Telegram user id — callback data is never trusted for permissions.
    Example: /adminpanel

GROUP CONFIGURATION (admin)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/setchat chat_id [setting] [on|off]
    Per-group feature toggles. Stored centrally in MongoDB; defaults are all
    ON. Settings:
        group        — enable/disable the whole bot in that chat
        economy      — economy commands (/bal /pay /profile /bank /stock etc.)
        games        — /fly /mines /bet
        leaderboard  — /leader
        admin        — admin commands (owner bypasses this toggle)

    When a feature is OFF the bot stays silent (or shows a short notice)
    for those commands in that group only.
    Usage:
        /setchat [setting] [on|off]          (inside the group)
        /setchat chat_id [setting] [on|off]  (from DM)
        /setchat chat_id                     (view a chat's config from DM)
        /setchat                             (view current group config)
    Examples:
        /setchat games off                      (in the group)
        /setchat -1001234567890 economy off     (from DM)
        /setchat -1001234567890                 (view from DM)
