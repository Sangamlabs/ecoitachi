================================================================================
 UNOITACHI BOT — COMPLETE COMMAND REFERENCE
 (usage, explanation, limits and current defaults for every command)
================================================================================

GENERAL NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Currency is shown as "₹ UN". Internally money is stored in integer sub-units
  where 100 units = ₹1, so all amounts are whole numbers. Example: /pay @user 500
  means 500 units = ₹5.00.
- Every user is auto-created on first interaction. New users receive a
  welcome grant of ₹500 (50,000 units) in their wallet (admin-configurable).
- Most commands work in private chats, groups AND supergroups. Channels and
  bots are ignored.
- Reply-based target resolution: replying to a user's message is the most
  reliable way to target them; otherwise you can pass @username or a numeric
  Telegram user id.
- All money movements produce an audit transaction with a unique #txid.
  /transactions only shows your last 10 transfer (PAY) logs; other types
  are still recorded in the database.

PERMISSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two ranks control the bot:
  1. OWNER      — the numeric OWNER_ID set in .env. Full access to everything,
                  and bypasses per-group "admin commands off" restrictions.
  2. SUDO ADMIN — users added by the owner with /addsudo. Access to all admin
                  commands except /addsudo and /rsudo.

Permissions are ALWAYS resolved from numeric Telegram user ids, never
usernames, so a username change cannot grant or revoke access.

Group admins on Telegram (group admin rights) have NO special bot powers.

PART 1 — USER COMMANDS (everyone)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BASICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/start
    Welcome message. Registers you in the economy if new (grants the ₹500
    welcome balance). Private chat only.

/help
    Shows a short summary of user commands.

ECONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/profile
    Your full profile: name, user id, wallet, bank, net worth, stock value,
    total earned, total spent, current leaderboard rank.

/bal [@user | user_id]
    Balance of wallet, bank and net worth. With no argument shows your own.
    Reply to a user's message (with or without argument) to see their balance.
    Example:  /bal
              /bal @someone          /bal 123456789

/pay @user amount | /pay user_id amount | reply to a user + /pay amount
    Send money from YOUR WALLET to another user's wallet. Amount must be > 0
    and you must have enough in your wallet. Target priority is:
        reply-to user id  >  explicit numeric id  >  username lookup.
    The receiver gets a private notification. Logs PAY transaction.
    Example:  /pay @sangam 5000          (₹50.00)
              /pay 123456789 5000
              (reply to a message) /pay 5000

/leader
    Top-10 net-worth leaderboard (wallet + bank + live stock value).

FREE REWARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/daily     Claim free money. Default ₹500, cooldown 24h.
/weekly    Claim free money. Default ₹3,000, cooldown 7 days.
/monthly   Claim free money. Default ₹12,000, cooldown 30 days.

    The three rewards have independent cooldowns — you can claim all three.
    Amounts are set by admins via /setreward. While on cooldown you are told
    how many seconds remain.

BANK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/bank
    Bank overview: your bank balance, wallet, interest rate, withdrawal tax,
    and the size of the tax pool.

/deposit amount
    Move money wallet -> bank. Earns interest. Bank balance cannot be spent
    on games/payments until withdrawn. Example: /deposit 10000 (₹100.00)

/withdraw amount
    Move money bank -> wallet. A withdrawal tax (default 5%) is charged and
    goes into the tax pool, which is redistributed monthly to the Top-10
    earners. Example: /withdraw 10000

/transactions
    Your last 10 TRANSFER logs — money sent to you or sent by you.
    Sent transfers show "PAY →", received transfers show "PAY ←" with the
    txid. All other types (deposits, game wins, rewards, ...) are not shown
    here. Example:
        PAY SENT → ₹20.00 · #a1b2c3d4e5
        PAY RECEIVED ← ₹5.00 · #f6e7d8c9b0

MARKET (stocks)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Assets (with volatility): BTC Bitcoin, ETH Ethereum, RSX RS Index,
SOL Solana, DOGE Dogecoin. Prices tick periodically on the configured
volatility model. You can buy fractional amounts.

/stocklist
    Market overview: every asset, current price and 24h change.

/stock SYMBOL
    Asset detail: price, 24h high/low, volatility. Example: /stock BTC

/buystock SYMBOL quantity
    Buy fractional shares from your wallet. Example: /buystock BTC 0.01
    Logs STOCK_BUY transaction.

/sellstock SYMBOL quantity
    Sell shares you own back to your wallet at current price.
    Example: /sellstock BTC 0.005. Logs STOCK_SELL transaction.

/portfolio
    Your holdings, current value and total profit/loss.

ASSET MARKET (user)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Assets are a SECOND market, fully independent from stocks. Examples included
on first run: LAPT (Luxury Apartment), M5 (BMW M5), GOLD (Gold Reserve,
fractional), OIL (Crude Oil, fractional), BOND (Government Bond), DIAMOND
(Rare Diamond). Prices tick on their own volatility schedule.

/assets [page]
    Asset market overview: every listed asset, price and 24h change.
    Example: /assets 2

/asset SYMBOL
    Asset detail: category, price, 24h high/low, volatility, quantity rules,
    description. Example: /asset GOLD

/buyasset SYMBOL quantity
    Buy from the market. You are shown a confirmation with the current price
    and a CONFIRM button; the price is re-checked server-side when you press
    it (if it changed you must try again — you are never charged a stale
    price). Logs ASSET_BUY. Example: /buyasset GOLD 0.5

/sellasset SYMBOL quantity
    Sell owned quantity back to the market at the current sell price.
    Logs ASSET_SELL. Example: /sellasset GOLD 0.25

/myassets
    Your asset portfolio: each holding (quantity, unit cost, live value, P/L)
    plus totals and overall P/L.

/assetstats
    Market statistics: total/active assets, total market value, trading
    volume, gainers/losers/flat count.

RESALE MARKET (user) — your own listings
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can SELL assets you own to other users at any price you choose. Each
listing gets its own unique LISTING ID (e.g. LST-1A2B3C4D) — it is never the
asset's ID. Buyers buy the WHOLE listing (all of its quantity).

/listasset SYMBOL quantity price
    Create a listing offering quantity of SYMBOL at "price" per unit.
    Your quantity is reserved in the listing (it stays in your holdings but
    cannot be sold elsewhere until the listing closes). A configurable
    listing fee (default 0) is charged. Logs the fee (if any).
    Example: /listasset GOLD 0.5 82000

/listings [SYMBOL] [page]
    Browse the active resale market. Optionally filter by SYMBOL and paginate
    with ◀ ▶ buttons. Example: /listings GOLD

/buylisting LISTING_ID
    Buy an entire listing. The money moves from your wallet to the seller's
    wallet and the asset moves to your holdings atomically. Logs
    ASSET_LISTING_BUY (buyer) and ASSET_LISTING_SALE (seller).
    You cannot buy your own listing. Example: /buylisting LST-1A2B3C4D

/mylistings
    All your listings with their current status (active/pending/sold/cancelled).

/cancellisting LISTING_ID
    Cancel one of your ACTIVE listings; the quantity is released back to you.
    Example: /cancellisting LST-1A2B3C4D

GAMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All games require money in your WALLET (bet is locked atomically), and every
game has a cooldown (default 60s). You can only have ONE active session per
game. A failed bet only loses the bet; no money is ever duplicated.

/fly low|medium|high amount
    Plane game. Choose difficulty, bet an amount. Win probability and
    multiplier depend on the difficulty (see /flyset table below).
    Example: /fly high 1000

/mines amount
    Minesweeper-style 6x6 board with a configurable number of bombs (default
    5). A bet is locked; reveal safe tiles to increase the payout multiplier,
    or "cash out" early. Hit a bomb and you lose the bet. Sessions expire
    after 5 minutes (default) and are settled as a loss. Played via an
    inline keyboard (buttons). Example: /mines 2000

/bet amount
    Instant coin-flip bet with a fixed win probability and multiplier
    (default 50% / 2.0x). Example: /bet 1000

CRIME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/rob @user | /rob user_id | reply to a user + /rob
    Try to rob another user's BANK (not wallet). Uses the reply-based target
    resolution. Outcome is random:
        - success: you steal a percentage of the victim's bank, clamped to a
          minimum/maximum (default 10%, min ₹1, max ₹5,000);
        - fail:    the police catch you and you get nothing.
    Either way the 60s cooldown applies. The victim is sent a private
    notification. Robber and victim both get audit transactions (ROB/ROBBED).
    Example:  /rob @sangam
              (reply to a message) /rob
    You cannot rob yourself, and the victim must have money in their bank.

PART 2 — ADMIN COMMANDS (owner + sudo)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Target resolution (for @user arguments everywhere):
    reply-to message  >  @username  >  numeric user id.
The target user must already have started the bot.

PERMISSIONS (owner only — works in DM and groups)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/addsudo @user | /addsudo user_id | reply to a user + /addsudo
    Make a user a sudo admin. They get access to every admin command except
    /addsudo and /rsudo.
    In DM: target by @username or numeric user id.
    In a group: reply to the user's message (or @username / numeric id).
    Example:  /addsudo @sangam
              /addsudo 123456789
              (reply to a message) /addsudo

/rsudo @user | /rsudo user_id | reply to a user + /rsudo
    Remove a sudo admin (same target rules as /addsudo). The owner cannot be
    removed.

HELP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/adminhelp
    Shows this admin command reference inside Telegram.

ECONOMY — MONEY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/give @user amount
    Add money to a user's wallet. Amount must be > 0. Counts toward the
    user's total earned and monthly earnings (feeds the monthly ranking).
    Logs ADMIN_GIVE. Example: /give @sangam 50000

/remove @user amount
    Take money from a user's wallet. Uses an atomic balance guard, so it can
    never make a balance negative — fails with "Insufficient balance" if the
    user cannot cover it. Logs ADMIN_REMOVE.

/getcoin amount
    Admin cheat: instantly credit YOUR OWN wallet with money. Logs an
    ADMIN_GIVE transaction against you.
    Example: /getcoin 100000   (₹1,000.00)

BANK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/setinterest rate
    Bank interest percentage paid per 24 hours. Valid 0-100. Default 2%.
    Example: /setinterest 3

/settax rate
    Withdrawal tax percentage. Valid 0-100. Default 5%. Tax is collected into
    the pool, not destroyed, and redistributed monthly. Example: /settax 4

/banksettings
    Show current interest rate, withdrawal tax and tax pool size.

GAMES — FLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/flyset low|medium|high field value
    Change ONE fly difficulty setting.
    Fields:  min_mult | max_mult | risk | win_prob | cooldown | min_bet | max_bet
    Validation:
        min_mult/max_mult : 0 to 1000, and min_mult <= max_mult
        risk              : percent, 0 to 100
        win_prob          : 0.0 to 1.0
        min_bet/max_bet   : >= 0, and min_bet <= max_bet
        cooldown          : seconds, >= 0
    Example: /flyset high win_prob 0.40

    Current defaults per difficulty (cooldown 60s everywhere):
        low     : min_mult 1.1, max_mult 1.6, risk 20%, win 75%, bet ₹1 - ₹1,000
        medium  : min_mult 1.5, max_mult 2.5, risk 35%, win 55%, bet ₹1 - ₹2,500
        high    : min_mult 2.0, max_mult 5.0, risk 50%, win 35%, bet ₹1 - ₹5,000

/flytrap difficulty min_mult max_mult risk win_prob cooldown min_bet max_bet
    Overwrite ALL settings of one difficulty in a single command (same
    validation as /flyset).
    Example: /flytrap high 2.0 6.0 0.5 0.30 60 100 600000

GAMES — BET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/betset win_prob multiplier min_bet max_bet [cooldown]
    Configure the coin-flip game. win_prob is 0.0-1.0, multiplier 0-1000,
    min_bet <= max_bet, cooldown in seconds (optional).
    Defaults: win 0.5, multiplier 2.0, bet ₹1 - ₹1,000, cooldown 60s.

GAMES — MINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/minestrap bombs min_reveals min_bet max_bet cooldown duration
    Mines tuning. Board is 6x6 = 36 tiles.
        bombs        : 1 to 34
        min_reveals  : 1 to (36 - bombs)
        min_bet/max_bet : >= 0, min_bet <= max_bet
        cooldown     : seconds >= 0
        duration     : seconds >= 30 (session expiry = auto-loss)
    Defaults: 5 bombs, 3 min reveals, bet ₹1 - ₹2,000, cooldown 60s,
    duration 300s (5 minutes).

/minestrap multipliers 1.0,1.2,...
    Set a custom payout table (comma separated, up to 17 entries — one per
    reveal; the first entry is the 1st-reveal multiplier).

/minestrap multipliers auto
    Restore the built-in payout table:
    1.0, 1.18, 1.4, 1.66, 2.0, 2.45, 3.0, 3.8, 4.9, 6.5, 9.0, 13.0, 19.0,
    30.0, 50.0, 85.0, 150.0

GAMES — ROB (crime tuning)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/robset field value
    Configure /rob. Fields and defaults:
        win_prob  : 0.0-1.0 (default 0.50)  chance the heist succeeds
        percent   : 0-100   (default 10%)   share of victim's bank stolen
        min       : >= 0    (default ₹1)    minimum stolen amount
        max       : >= 0    (default ₹5,000) maximum stolen amount (min <= max)
        cooldown  : seconds (default 60)
    Example: /robset win_prob 0.60

MARKET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/addstock SYMBOL name base_price volatility
    List a NEW asset on the market (or re-list one that was removed).
        SYMBOL    : letters/numbers only, shown in UPPERCASE
        name      : display name (may contain spaces, put it right after SYMBOL)
        base_price: opening price, must be > 0
        volatility: random-walk strength, must be between 0 and 1
    The price starts at base_price and is ticked by the scheduler like every
    other asset. Re-listing an inactive symbol restores it.
    Example: /addstock ADA Cardano 45000 0.02

/rmstock SYMBOL
    Delist an asset from the market. Buying, selling and price ticks stop for
    it immediately. Existing holdings are kept but can no longer be traded.
    Example: /rmstock DOGE

ASSET MARKET (admin)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Categories: REAL_ESTATE | VEHICLE | BUSINESS | GOLD | BOND | COMMODITY |
LUXURY | COLLECTIBLE | DIGITAL | OTHER.

/addasset SYMBOL name CATEGORY base_price volatility
    List a NEW asset on the market.
        SYMBOL     : letters/numbers only, shown in UPPERCASE (1-10 chars)
        name       : display name (may contain spaces)
        CATEGORY   : one of the categories above
        base_price : opening price, must be > 0
        volatility : random-walk strength, 0 to 1
    The asset gets a unique Asset ID (AST-XXXXXXXX) and is ticked by the
    scheduler. Logs ADD_ASSET.
    Example: /addasset VILA Grand Villa REAL_ESTATE 20000000 0.015
    Optional per-asset rules: /editasset SYMBOL allow_fractional true,
    min_quantity, max_quantity, quantity_step.

/editasset SYMBOL field value
    Edit ONE editable field of an asset.
    Fields: name, description, category, emoji, base_price, price,
    volatility, min_quantity, max_quantity, quantity_step, allow_fractional,
    is_tradeable, is_active.
    Setting base_price also resets the current price. Setting price updates
    change/change_percent and writes a price-history point. Logs EDIT_ASSET.
    Example: /editasset VILA name 'Grand Luxury Villa'

/assetset SYMBOL field value
    Same as /editasset but logged as ASSET_CONFIG_CHANGE (use it for
    operational tweaks like allow_fractional / is_tradeable).

/assetprice SYMBOL price
    Manually set an asset's current price. Recomputes change/change_percent
    and writes a price-history point. Logs MANUAL_PRICE_CHANGE.
    Example: /assetprice GOLD 82000

/assetvolatility SYMBOL value
    Set an asset's volatility (0 to 1). Logs VOLATILITY_CHANGE.
    Example: /assetvolatility GOLD 0.02

/rmasset SYMBOL
    Delist an asset. Trading and price ticks stop; existing holders keep
    their holdings but can no longer trade them. Logs REMOVE_ASSET.

/restoreasset SYMBOL
    Re-list a previously delisted asset (keeps its price history).
    Logs RESTORE_ASSET.

/assetinfo SYMBOL
    Full asset detail (works for delisted assets too, shows a DELISTED flag).

/assetlist [page]
    All assets with price and category (20 per page). Pass "all" as the first
    argument to include delisted assets.

/assetsearch query
    Search assets by symbol, name or category.
    Example: /assetsearch gold

/assetowners SYMBOL [page]
    Top holders of an asset by current value: username/id, quantity, value.

/assetadminstats
    Admin market stats: active/total/delisted, holders, holdings, total
    value, total invested, total P/L, trading volume, top assets by value,
    most-held assets.

LISTING MARKET (admin)

/listinginfo LISTING_ID
    Full listing report: seller, buyer, status, quantity, unit price, total.
    Example: /listinginfo LST-1A2B3C4D

/rmlisting LISTING_ID

FREE REWARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/setreward daily|weekly|monthly amount
    Set the free-currency claim amount for a reward. Amount must be > 0.
    Cooldowns are fixed to the reward name (daily = 24h, weekly = 7 days,
    monthly = 30 days).
    Defaults: daily ₹500, weekly ₹3,000, monthly ₹12,000.
    Example: /setreward daily 75000   (₹750)

USERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/freeze @user
    Freeze a user. They keep their money but cannot spend it (games, pay,
    rob, withdraw are blocked). Their balance stays intact.

/unfreeze @user
    Remove a freeze.

/ban @user
    Full economy ban: the user cannot use any economy command at all.
    You cannot ban yourself.

/unban @user
    Remove a ban.

/userinfo @user
    Full account report: wallet, bank, net worth, total earned/spent,
    monthly rank, transaction count, and BANNED/FROZEN badges.

STATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/econstats
    Economy-wide stats: user count, total money in circulation, total banked,
    tax pool size, total transactions, number of active stock assets.

GROUP CONFIGURATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/setchat [setting] [on|off]          (inside a group)
/setchat chat_id [setting] [on|off]  (from DM)
/setchat chat_id                     (view a chat's config from DM)
/setchat                             (view current group config from inside)

    Per-group feature toggles. Stored centrally in MongoDB; defaults are all
    ON. Settings:
        group        — enable/disable the whole bot in that chat
        economy      — economy commands (/bal /pay /profile /bank /stock etc.)
        games        — /fly /mines /bet
        leaderboard  — /leader
        admin        — admin commands (owner bypasses this toggle)

    When a feature is OFF the bot stays silent (or shows a short notice)
    for those commands in that group only.
    Examples:
        /setchat games off                      (in the group)
        /setchat -1001234567890 economy off     (from DM)
        /setchat -1001234567890                 (view from DM)
